Privacy

Who is responsible

Federico Consorte — federico.consorte@mail.polimi.it

What is collected

When you visit this site, a few details about the visit are recorded: approximate city, region and country, the network you connect from (for example your internet provider or organisation), browser and operating system, the page that linked here, and a random visitor code that changes every day.

This information is used for statistics on visits to the site, on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your IP address

Your IP address is used only for a moment, to work out the approximate location and network, and is not stored. The daily visitor code is derived from it in a way that cannot be reversed.

Service providers

The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). Visits are processed through Cloudflare and the resulting summary is delivered to me via Telegram. These providers handle data under their own privacy policies and may process it outside the European Union. No data is sold or used for advertising.

Cookies

This site uses no cookies and no advertising or profiling tools. If you use the light/dark switch, your choice is saved in your browser only.

Retention

Visit summaries are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to access or delete data about you, or object to its processing, by writing to the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
